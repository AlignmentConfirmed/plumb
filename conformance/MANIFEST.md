# CONFORMANCE CORPUS

Bytes on disk and the verdict each must produce. Generated by
`cargo test --test conformance -- --ignored write`, and checked
against the live codec by `every_case_behaves_as_the_manifest_says`
so neither can drift.

An implementation conforms when it produces the stated verdict for
every case. The verdicts are four, and the difference between the
last two is where most readers go wrong:

| verdict | meaning |
|---|---|
| `accept` | decodes to a whole record |
| `refuse` | malformed; a reader that accepts has a defect |
| `skip` | not a record this reader owns — step over it whole and continue |
| `wait` | a record has begun and not finished; hold, and neither accept nor refuse |

| case | verdict | bytes | why |
|---|---|---|---|
| `01-frame-empty` | `accept` | `0100000000` | a tag-1 record with no value is well formed |
| `02-relation-triangle` | `accept` | `0141000000030000000300000000000000010000000001000000010100000001…` | three orbs, three charged edges |
| `03-closures-one-grain` | `accept` | `33140000000100000003000000000000000100000002000000` | tag 51, one grain over three orbs |
| `04-closures-none` | `accept` | `330400000000000000` | a manifold with no closures still sends the frame |
| `05-unknown-tag` | `skip` | `c804000000deadbeef` | tag 200 is granted to nobody: skip the whole record, do not fail |
| `06-unknown-then-relation` | `skip` | `c804000000deadbeef0141000000030000000300000000000000010000000001…` | what follows an unknown record must still decode — the linking-mesh property |
| `07-truncated-header` | `wait` | `014100` | fewer than five bytes cannot carry a tag and a length |
| `08-truncated-value` | `wait` | `0141000000030000000300000000000000010000000001000000010100000001…` | the header declares more value than has arrived |
| `09-length-overruns` | `refuse` | `01ffffffff` | declares 4 GiB of value, past MAX_RECORD: refuse at the header, before any value is held |
| `15-length-at-the-bound` | `wait` | `0100001000` | exactly MAX_RECORD is permitted, so the reader waits for it |
| `16-length-one-past-the-bound` | `refuse` | `0101001000` | one byte past MAX_RECORD refuses — the bound is exact, not approximate |
| `10-ratio-canonical` | `accept` | `0001000000070100000005` | 7/5, reduced, sign 0 |
| `11-ratio-not-reduced` | `refuse` | `0001000000020100000004` | 2/4 must REFUSE, not be silently repaired to 1/2 |
| `12-ratio-zero-denominator` | `refuse` | `00010000000100000000` | an absence, not an infinity |
| `13-ratio-unknown-sign` | `refuse` | `0701000000010100000002` | the sign byte is 0 or 1; 7 is neither |
| `14-ratio-zero` | `accept` | `00000000000100000001` | zero's magnitude is the empty string, not a zero byte |

## The bound

Cases 09, 15 and 16 rest on `MAX_RECORD = 1 << 20`, one mebibyte of value. **Measured**: the largest record across 4127 stored netstratum chronicle records is 585 bytes, so the bound clears it by roughly 1790. See `IS-2` §7.3.

The bound is what makes *unsatisfiable* separable from *not yet arrived* — without one they are the same observation and a reader can only wait. A peer may agree a LARGER bound in the handshake and may not agree a smaller one silently.

## What this corpus does not cover

The session. Every case here is one record or two, hand-fed. Sequencing and freshness are `IS-2` §6, still unbuilt. The silent stall of §7 is resolved and cases 09, 15 and 16 carry it.

## X2/X4 — the settlement receipt (the facilitator's check)

| vector | file | what a facilitator must find |
|---|---|---|
| V20 | `20-receipt.bin` | `receipt bytes ‖ attestation(97)`: court `court-a`, epoch 5, a query_id, the hexagon's work_id, axes `[6, 6]`; the attestation is Ed25519 over BLAKE3 of the receipt bytes |
| V21 | `21-receipt-chain.bin` | the chain (IS-6) binding `court-a`'s key, epochs 0–100 |

**The whole check, in any language with BLAKE3 + Ed25519:** split V20
at `len − 97`; verify the attestation's signature over
BLAKE3(receipt bytes); replay V21 and resolve the signer's key to a
bound holder; require that holder to equal the receipt's named court
and the receipt's epoch to sit inside the bind window. Expected
verdict: **valid**. Tampering with any receipt byte must flip the
verdict to forged. Regeneration:
`cargo test -p plumb-datum --test market -- --ignored`.
